# 2. Customer journeys

Step by step, in the order a new customer meets them. **Bold** text is the exact wording
on screen, so you can say "tap the button that says…".

Most people arrive from the EELA site (eela.empowrcic.org). Its session pages link
straight into booking here.

---

## A. Create an account

1. Go to **members.empowrcic.org** → sign in page → **Create an account**.
2. Fill in **Your name**, **Email**, and a **Password**.
3. Answer **Who will be skating?**:
   - **I will be skating**
   - **A child or someone else will be skating**
   - **Both me and someone else**
4. Optional: tick **Keep me updated by email** for news and offers.
5. Press **Create account**. The screen says a confirmation link has been sent.
6. Open the email **"Confirm your email address"** and click the link. The account is
   now active and they land on **Your account**.

> **Didn't get the email?** Check spam or junk and wait a couple of minutes. Check
> they typed the email correctly. If it was mistyped, they sign up again with the
> right one.

## B. Signing in

The sign-in page has two tabs:

- **Password**: email and password, then **Sign in**.
- **Email me a link**: type the email, then **Send sign-in link**. The email "Your
  sign-in link" arrives, and one click signs them in. No password needed. This is the
  easiest option for anyone who has forgotten their password.

**Forgotten password:** on the Password tab, press **Forgotten your password?**, enter
the email, then **Send the link**. The email "Choose a new password" arrives. The link
**expires in one hour**. The screen always says the link was sent, even for an email
with no account. That is deliberate, for privacy. If nothing arrives, the email probably
isn't registered. Try the sign-in link option, or sign up.

If **Email me a link** says **"No account found with that email — please sign up
first."**, they need to create an account (A).

## C. Add the skaters (household)

On **Your account**, under **Your household**:

- **Add myself as a skater**: for the account holder.
- **Add a child or someone else**: for anyone else.

For each skater:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name, Date of birth | Age and eligibility are worked out from date of birth |
| Emergency contact name and phone | For a child, the parent or guardian. It's pre-filled with the account holder's details. For an adult adding **themselves**, it must be **someone else**. It is never pre-filled with their own details. |
| Medical notes | Allergies and conditions coaches should know. Shown to staff on the register. |
| How will this child usually go home? | Under-18s only. Pre-fills the departure question at booking. It can be changed for each session. |

Skaters can be edited later with **Edit**, or removed.

## D. Sign the waiver

While anyone in the household is unsigned, a red notice stays at the top of the account
page naming them, with a **Complete the waiver** link.

1. **Complete the waiver** (or go to /waiver).
2. Under **Who does this cover?**, tick everyone to cover. Several people can be done
   at once.
3. Fill in the emergency contact, contact number, and **Relationship to skater(s)**.
4. Tick all three agreements: **I agree to the terms and conditions**, **I agree to the
   risk waiver**, **I consent to photo and filming** (each has a link to read the
   document).
5. **Save waiver**. The screen says "Welcome to the Sk8Fam!".

Once per person. It never needs doing again for a new session.

## E. Book and pay (the basket)

1. Open a session (from EELA, or members.empowrcic.org/sessions). Pick a **date**.
   For a course, pick **Book this course**.
2. Tick **who is coming**. Anyone greyed out shows the reason next to their name:
   - **"— this session is …"**: outside the age range.
   - **"— already covered by …"**: they have a subscription for this session. Nothing
     to pay.
   - **"— waiver needed"**: sign the waiver first (D).
3. **For each child**, there's a box: **Leaving unaccompanied after this session**.
   Leave it **off** if the child is being collected in person, which is the default.
   Only turn it on if they have permission to leave on their own. Then a short
   checklist has to be finished.
4. **Roller Quad Camp only:** choose skates or hire and protective gear for each child.
5. **Early bird**: where a date still has early-bird tickets, a **Choose your ticket**
   box shows **Early bird** and **Standard**. If there aren't enough early-bird
   tickets for everyone selected, Early bird is greyed out with an explanation.
6. Press **Add to basket**. They can add more sessions the same way (up to **10
   places** in one basket).
7. Open the **basket** (the basket icon, which shows a count). Check each booking. Use
   **Edit** to change one, or the bin icon to remove it.
8. Press **Continue to payment**. The app checks and holds **all** the places together.
   If any one of them isn't available, **none** are held and nothing is charged.
9. Pay on the Stripe card page. Apple Pay and Google Pay work where the phone supports
   them.
10. They return to **Booking confirmed** ("…paid — see you there!"), with **View my
    bookings** and **Book another session**. A confirmation email with the ticket
    link(s) follows.

> If they see **"Confirming your booking…"**, the payment arrived and the app is
> finishing up. The page refreshes itself. If they see **"This payment wasn't
> completed"**, no money was taken and their basket is still there to try again.

## F. Tickets and check-in

- Each skater gets their own **ticket**: a page with a QR code, the date, the venue,
  and the amount paid. Find it from the email, or **My bookings → View ticket**.
- At the door, **staff scan the QR code**. If there's no phone or the battery is flat,
  staff find them by name on the register. Nothing needs printing, but **Save / Print**
  is there for anyone who wants it.
- Subscribers **don't need a ticket**. They're on the register automatically.

## G. My bookings: cancel or move a date

**My bookings** lists **Upcoming** and **Past**, with a status on each:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Payment pending | They started paying and haven't finished. The place is held for about 30 minutes. |
| Confirmed | Paid and booked |
| Attended | Checked in at the door |
| Cancelled — refunded | Cancelled, with money going back to the card |
| Cancelled — credited | Empowr cancelled the session and gave a credit instead |
| Cancelled | Cancelled with nothing to refund (e.g. an unpaid hold that expired) |
| No-show | Didn't attend |

**Cancel** (where the session allows it): **Cancel booking** → it shows the refund amount
→ **Cancel and refund**. The refund goes to the card they paid with, usually within
**5–10 working days**. A cancellation email is sent.

**Move to another date** (where the session allows it): **Move to another date** → pick
a date → **Move booking**. There's no charge and no refund. **The same ticket and QR code
now show the new date.** A booking can be moved **once**.

If a child's booking had "Leaving unaccompanied" set, the member is told to give that
permission **again for the new date**. It doesn't carry over, so until they do, staff
will expect the child to be collected in person.

If a button isn't there, or pressing it shows a reason, the rules in
[03-policies.md](./03-policies.md) apply. The most common reasons: the session is less
than 48 hours away, it's a non-refundable session, or it's a course.

## H. Subscriptions (monthly)

**Starting one:**

1. On the session's page, find **Coming every week?**, which shows the monthly price, and press **Subscribe**.
2. Sign in if needed, then choose **Who is this subscription for?** and press
   **Subscribe**.
3. Pay on the Stripe page. They return to **Membership** with **"Your subscription is
   set up. Your place is held every week — just turn up, you do not need to book."**

Rules they'll meet on that page:

- One subscription = **one skater, one weekly session**.
- A waiver is needed first. Unsigned skaters are named with a link.
- **"Nobody in your household is in the age range for this session."**: age.
- **"Everyone in your household who can attend this session is already subscribed to
  it."**: already done.
- **Skate Jam is seasonal (3 September – 25 March).** The subscription pauses
  automatically outside the season and restarts itself. No payment is taken in
  between, and there's nothing to cancel.

**Managing one:** **Membership** lists each subscription with a badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | All good |
| Payment failed — update your card | Their card was declined. They should update it through **Manage or cancel**. |
| Cancelled — runs until *date* | They cancelled. They're still covered until the end of the paid month. |

**Manage or cancel** opens Stripe's secure billing page, where they can change card,
see invoices, or cancel.

A subscriber who tries to book and pay for a session their subscription already covers
is stopped ("…already covered by … so we won't charge you twice").
