| 2026-08-26 | **Subscriptions are per PARTICIPANT, not per household** — `mem_memberships.participant_id`, `ON DELETE RESTRICT` | Capacity and coach-to-child ratios are counted per child; a household plan at £30 covering three children undercuts the per-child economics the pricing assumes. RESTRICT because deleting the row would NOT stop Stripe billing. | Accepted (Empowr) |
| 2026-08-26 | **A Subscription is to ONE WEEKLY SLOT (day + time), not to an offering** — `mem_plan_entitlements.weekday` + `starts_at_local`, NULL = every slot | Sk8 Skool for Kidz is £30 **per slot**; Mon 16:00 and Wed 17:00 are separate plans and a child doing both pays £60/month. Plans went 4 → 5. | Accepted (Empowr) |
| 2026-08-26 | Slot identity is **day + time, NOT venue** | The Wednesday Kidz slot runs at 17:00 all year but relocates seasonally (Sports Field outdoors Apr-Aug, Honor Oak indoors). Venue therefore cannot identify it, so the "split the offering when venues differ" precedent used for Prep to Street L1/L2 does not apply. | Accepted |
| 2026-08-26 | Slot matching compares in **Europe/London**, in a pure `lib/slot-matching.ts` | Occurrence times are `timestamptz` but mean UK wall-clock.
