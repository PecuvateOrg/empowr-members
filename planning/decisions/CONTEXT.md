| 2026-08-26 | **Subscriptions are per PARTICIPANT, not per household** — `mem_memberships.participant_id`, `ON DELETE RESTRICT` | Capacity and coach-to-child ratios are counted per child; a household plan at £30 covering three children undercuts the per-child economics the pricing assumes. RESTRICT because deleting the row would NOT stop Stripe billing. | Accepted (Empowr) |
| 2026-08-26 | **A Subscription is to ONE WEEKLY SLOT (day + time), not to an offering** — `mem_plan_entitlements.weekday` + `starts_at_local`, NULL = every slot | Sk8 Skool for Kidz is £30 **per slot**; Mon 16:00 and Wed 17:00 are separate plans and a child doing both pays £60/month. Plans went 4 → 5. | Accepted (Empowr) |
| 2026-08-26 | Slot identity is **day + time, NOT venue** | The Wednesday Kidz slot runs at 17:00 all year but relocates seasonally (Sports Field outdoors Apr-Aug, Honor Oak indoors). Venue therefore cannot identify it, so the "split the offering when venues differ" precedent used for Prep to Street L1/L2 does not apply. | Accepted |
| 2026-08-26 | Slot matching compares in **Europe/London**, in a pure `lib/slot-matching.ts` | Occurrence times are `timestamptz` but mean UK wall-clock. A UTC comparison shifts the hour — and for late sessions the weekday — across the BST boundary, so a "Mondays 16:00" slot would match all summer and silently stop each October. | Accepted |
| 2026-08-26 | Stripe Prices referenced by **`lookup_key`, never Price ID**; `stripe_price_id` held NULL by CHECK | One Supabase project serves every environment, but production runs Stripe LIVE and previews/local run TEST — a stored Price ID is correct in exactly one of them. A lookup_key is identical in both, and re-pricing needs no DB change. | Accepted |
| 2026-08-26 | **Webhook ownership resolved once, at dispatch, default-deny** (both apps) | The Stripe account is shared with Heroes and Stripe delivers every subscribed event type to every endpoint on it; delivery is not scoped by API key. Per-branch guards failed twice. Heroes identifies structurally by Product ID; Members by `subscription_data.metadata.app`. | Accepted |

| 2026-08-31 | **No session cap — `sessions_per_period` stays NULL** (Q3) | £30/month already prices in 4.33 sessions (52 weeks ÷ 12), so a cap of 4 would charge for 4.33 and deliver 4 in the ~4 months a year with five occurrences — £7.50 per session against £6.92. It also **contradicts the Q5 decision below**: if the place is reserved indefinitely, it is reserved all five times, and a cap means refusing a child at the door from a place they hold. The alternative considered and rejected was capping at 4 with the price cut to ~£27.70. | Accepted (Empowr, 2026-08-31) |
| 2026-08-31 | **A subscription reserves the weekly place indefinitely — no re-booking, ever** (Q5) | This is the whole incentive to subscribe: turn up and be checked in, with nothing to remember. ⚠️ **Build consequence, not a config flag:** capacity, the waiver gate and the door register all key off a booking row, so the system must CREATE those rows on the member's behalf for every occurrence in their slot. Needs to handle a lapsed waiver, a full session, and the Q8 season pause. Largest single piece of Steps 4–6. The earlier "one-tap reserve my place" compromise is explicitly rejected. | Accepted (Empowr, 2026-08-31) |
| 2026-08-31 | **On final payment failure: warn the member, revert to pay-as-you-go** (Q6) | Reverting is existing behaviour — entitlements already drop at `past_due`. The warning is new. Split by responsibility rather than duplicated: Stripe's own failed-payment email covers the per-attempt "update your card" nudge (it owns the retry timing and carries a working payment-update link), and Members builds ONE branded email for the terminal event, which is the only part Stripe cannot express — that the reserved place is gone and how to resubscribe. ⚠️ Enabling Stripe's toggle is **account-level**, so Heroes donors start receiving it too; that happens to close a real gap there (Heroes currently alerts staff only). | Accepted (Empowr, 2026-08-31) |
| 2026-08-31 | **Skate Jam out of season: pause collection and auto-resume** (Q8) | Stripe's `pause_collection` takes a `resumes_at`, so season end can set the September resume date in the same call — no member action, no resubscribe. Unblocks activating the Skate Jam plan, which was the only one gated on this. | Accepted (Empowr, 2026-08-31) |
| 2026-08-27 | Dunning end-of-retry action left at **Cancel the subscription** | Account-level with **no per-app override**, so it applies to Heroes donors too. Members separately pauses entitlements from the first failed payment. Consequence: a member whose card fails through the retry window loses their slot and must resubscribe. ✅ **VERIFIED IN THE DASHBOARD 2026-08-31** (no API exposes it): subscription status → *cancel the subscription*; invoice status → *leave the invoice past-due*; retries → **4 attempts over 2 weeks**. | Accepted (verified) |
| 2026-08-31 | Retry schedule reduced from Stripe's recommended **8 attempts / 2 weeks to 4 / 2 weeks** | Retries themselves are silent, so fewer attempts only trades recovery rate for nothing — *unless* failed-payment emails are on, and the Q6 decision turns them on. Each failed attempt then sends the member an email, so 8 would mean 8 emails in a fortnight. 4 is the humane number once emails are enabled, and the two settings must be reasoned about together. Side benefit: Heroes alerts staff on every `invoice.payment_failed`, so this halves that noise too. | Accepted (Empowr) |
| 2026-08-31 | Unpaid invoices **left past-due** rather than marked uncollectible | ⚠️ Recorded as the current setting, not as a considered decision. Consequence: after the subscription cancels, the unpaid invoice stays open indefinitely and keeps counting as a receivable that will never be collected. Low stakes at current volume, but *mark uncollectible* is the cleaner treatment for Xero reconciliation. Revisit when subscriptions carry real volume. | Current setting — not yet decided |
